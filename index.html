❓ Quiz Instruction Guide

Challenge Types

Expect three formats of questions during your run:

  • Single Choice: Click the matching button or use keys 1 to 4.
  • Multiple Answers: Check all boxes that apply and click Submit Answer.
  • Fill in the Blanks: Enter the precise missing text and click Submit Answer or press Enter.

Tactical Lifelines

Use your lifelines wisely (each can be used once per quiz):

  • 50:50: Removes 2 incorrect answers. (Only works on Single Choice).
  • +15s Time: Grants extra time to resolve the current card.
  • Hint: Displays a helpful clue in the question card. Note: Using a hint reduces the score obtainable for that question.

Scoring Algorithm

Points are computed based on speed and difficulty: Easy = 100 max, Medium = 200 max, Hard = 300 max. Quick responses yield higher points! In untimed mode, correct answers score flat points (Easy: 50, Medium: 100, Hard: 150).
